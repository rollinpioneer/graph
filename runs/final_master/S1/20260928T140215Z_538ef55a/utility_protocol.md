# Utility protocol (frozen before S1 analysis)

For a legal relation opportunity, HELPFUL means the relation-linked choice preserves the signed goal and independently reduces verified cost, rework, or failure risk under the same continuation; HARMFUL means it causes a verified adverse consequence; NEUTRAL means the verified consequence is indistinguishable within the predeclared tolerance; UNKNOWN means no independent consequence witness. Utility is separate from truth and from provider admission.

No extra move is a benefit by itself. No planner score, success label, or provider confidence is utility evidence.
