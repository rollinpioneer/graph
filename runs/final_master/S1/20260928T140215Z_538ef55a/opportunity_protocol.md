# Opportunity protocol (frozen before S1 analysis)

A case has a relation opportunity only if at least two legal candidates are available under the same public FactStore/contract/deadline, at least one nominal patch differs, the candidate can enter the finite relation-aware readout/tie-break path, and the relation can change a legal choice within the fixed 0.1*d_ref soft tie window.

Classify separately: no candidate patch; relation rejected at admission; candidate unreachable; hard-mask block; zero encoder footprint; zero readout/constant score; and missing independent consequence. Empty or constant relation input is retained in the denominator and is not silently converted to an opportunity.
