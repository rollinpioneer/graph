# C1-PUBLIC-DEPOTS-REL-V1: claim boundary

## Supported (one training seed per model; Track B is a project-defined split of public-generator instances; Track A is the 22 public IPC problems; 300 s per-problem budget)
1. With the relation-restricted goal attention trained under this recipe (REL-G), no advantage over the same module with full goal-to-goal attention (DENSE-G) was found on public Depots: equal completion on struct (32/32 both), fewer completions on the joint extrapolation set (119 vs 125 one-step, 116 vs 125 two-step, 119 vs 127 gated), symmetric paired cost differences on commonly solved problems (sign p 0.89, 0.77, 0.88), and a worse failure-penalised cost in every execution. On solved joint problems REL-G has a slightly lower mean ratio (0.005 to 0.022 lower), which does not compensate for the missing completions.
2. Goal-to-goal attention (dense or relation-restricted) gives cheaper plans than the no-attention scorer MG-G on commonly solved Track B problems (e.g. joint one-step: DENSE-G cheaper in 71, costlier in 16 problems). Within this experiment that gain is carried by the attention module, not by the relation restriction.
3. The selective look-ahead of the Blocksworld stage (rule and thresholds frozen from training margins only) transfers: on the joint set it solves at least as many problems as fixed two-step and produces cheaper plans for all three models with about 78% fewer leaf states; on IPC the saving is smaller (4% to 41%) and completions differ by at most one problem.
4. Blocksworld (development material): the gate keeps 254 of the 255 two-step completions with 76% fewer leaf states and no more avoidable destructions; a plain `h_add` leaf in the same tree is as good as or better than the learned leaf on every aggregate, which makes it a strong control for the learned value.
5. System context: WL-GOOSE (20/22, 128/128, 32/32) and LAMA (21/22 anytime) complete more problems than any neural row, especially on large IPC instances; on commonly solved Track B problems the neural rows produce cheaper plans than WL-GOOSE (e.g. REL-G one-step vs WL-GOOSE: cheaper in 72, costlier in 31 joint problems) and costlier plans than LAMA anytime.

## Not supported / must not be claimed
- That relation-restricted scoring generalises better, completes more, or reduces detours on Depots; also not the opposite as a general statement: one seed, one recipe, one minimal relation definition. In Depots the contract-threat term is empty and `direct` is activated only by the `on` atoms, so the mask may simply not be the right relation set for resource conflicts through hoists and trucks (the plan says this before the run). Nothing here shows that a different relation set, loss or seed would behave the same.
- That REL-G (or DENSE-G) is better than MG-G on completion: MG-G solved 119 / 123 / 126, DENSE-G 125 / 125 / 127, REL-G 119 / 116 / 119 joint problems; the MG-G vs attention comparison is confounded by the extra 66,088 parameters.
- Any invariance statement for the whole policy. The module-level probe only shows the mask is local (REL max change 2.4e-7 vs DENSE 1.31); the end-to-end augmentation probe shows REL, DENSE and MG all change centred scores by about 5% of the score range, so REL is not measurably more invariant.
- That the selective look-ahead is a finished method component: it was frozen on Blocksworld development material (Confirm128, used since the earlier cards), carried to Depots as registered, and measured with the same single seed; its Depots thresholds come from training margins; its advantage over fixed two-step on IPC is within one problem.
- That neural rows are competitive systems: they have contract access and bounded online search, WL-GOOSE and LAMA are full-search planners with different supervision and time use; the system table is not a structural comparison and no external method was weakened.
- Anything about trained-vs-untrained AIW-AD or KR 2023: neither was run (see disclosures).
- Statements about optimality on IPC for 15 of 22 problems: their reference length is the best known LAMA plan (an upper bound), not a proof. Ratios there can be below 1 (WL-GOOSE success-only ratio 0.969).
- Confirmation: Confirm128 is development material; Track B struct / joint / IPC were opened once after the checkpoint and threshold lock, but they are a single project-defined draw. p-values are uncorrected, from a few registered comparisons plus exploratory ones, and problems of one cell are not independent of each other in difficulty.
