## 1. Result in one paragraph

**Scientific label: `INCONCLUSIVE_CERTIFICATES`** (the registered mechanical output was `NO_LOCALIZED_SIGNAL_IN_OBSERVED_PREFIX`; see section 4 for why it was corrected). On the four IPC problems where DENSE is the weaker scorer (p06, p11, p12, p17) the DENSE searches show **no provable cross-group misorder, but the diagnostic could not have shown one**: the active reference anchor never got past reference position 1-6 (of 58 / 50 / 63 / 25 steps), so its known upper bound U stayed at 59 / 44 / 59 / 24, while the LM-cut lower bound of the popped states was 14-30; the best margin L(popped) - U(anchor) per problem is -30 / -20 / -29 / -8 (median -35 / -28 / -31 / -11) over the 117 events, and not one of 449 candidate pairs was decided in either direction. The same code does find cross-group witnesses when the labels are exact: on the four small Train96 controls the WL-driven searches have 10 / 5 / 10 / 2 certified cross-group witnesses (clearly inverted in 3 / 1 / 7 / 2 events), the DENSE-driven searches have none in 10 / 6 / 8 / 11 resolved events (of 11 / 7 / 11 / 12). So the card does **not** support layer 2, does **not** refute the cross-scope hypothesis for the IPC failures, and the neural-training proposal for it is rejected for now (plan 17.3). The WL backup stays unstarted.

## 2. Answers to the six questions (plan section 20)

**1. Which training constraints are really missing, which are only indirect?** The ACTION objective scores an action by l(s,a) = V(s) - V(T(s,a)); the shared term V(s) cancels in the softmax over the actions of one parent and in every difference of two action scores (formula fixture: constant shifts of V(s) change neither the probabilities nor the pairwise differences). So the loss has **no explicit parent-child term and no explicit term across different parents**. Parameter sharing, successors that are also sampled as other states, and the shared graph encoder constrain the values indirectly; this card did not train anything and therefore says nothing about how strong those indirect constraints are.

**2. Do provable cross-group misorders exist in the real OPEN, and where?** None was certified, and the search regions where they were looked for could not reveal any. F problems, DENSE-driven prefixes (4,096 expansions or 60 s): 30 / 30 / 29 / 28 snapshot events, 110 anchor pairs and 339 competitor pairs, **0 witnesses, 0 pairs decided as "popped state not worse", 449 unresolved**. All anchors exist (28-30 events each), but the deepest reference state ever in OPEN at a snapshot is position 1 / 6 / 4 / 1 of the reference plan, i.e. DENSE's frontier does not run along the (WL- or LAMA-produced) reference plan; the smallest U of the anchor is therefore 57 / 44 / 59 / 24, far above L of the popped states (9-30; median margin -35 / -28 / -31 / -11). The competitors have no finite U (only reference-path states and 28 bounded plans out of 64 requests provide one). R group (p14, p15): same picture (p15, whose reference is DENSE's own plan, reaches position 56 of 70 and the best margin is -1). Positive control with exact labels (Train96, d* known): WL-driven prefixes contain cross-group witnesses (10 / 5 / 10 / 2, sibling-pair witnesses 2 / 3 / 4 / 4 additionally), DENSE-driven prefixes contain none.

**3. Do local errors, chain order and cross-group witnesses coexist?** The registered local criterion (>= 6 of 16 sampled parents with a decision-level DENSE error) is not met: 1 / 1 / 1 / 2 parents on p06 / p11 / p12 / p17. Chain order R1 (parent vs its reference child) can be certified at 2 / 2 / 2 / 5 of 16 positions only (the lower bound equals the remaining reference length only near the goal); DENSE inverted 1 / 1 / 1 / 0 of them. Exploratory and outside the registered thresholds: among all pairs of labelled successors that bounds can order, DENSE is inverted on 14 of 85 on the four F problems (positions with >= 1 inversion: 2 / 1 / 1 / 3 of 16), on 0 of 42 on p14 / p15 and on 2 of 246 on the controls; WL on the F problems: 13 inverted, 38 correct, 34 numerical ties. These pair sets are biased (they pair the reference child with successors whose lower bound exceeds its suffix) and the counts are small. Whether local and cross-group errors coexist is **not decided** because the cross-group side has no coverage.

**4. How much is UNKNOWN, and what limits the references and bounds?** In the F problems 449 of 449 candidate pairs are unresolved (339 competitor pairs lack U; 110 anchor pairs have both bounds but they overlap). Limits: (a) one reference plan per problem, taken from other producers (shortest saved valid plan: 58 / 50 / 63 / 25 / 32 / 70 steps for p06 / p11 / p12 / p17 / p14 / p15; optimality unknown), so a state off that plan has no finite U unless a bounded plan was found (64 requests of 5 s, 28 plans); (b) LM-cut is loose: at the reference parents it is 0.69 / 0.70 / 0.65 / 0.83 / 0.71 / 0.63 of the remaining reference length on average (minimum 0.39-0.64); (c) the lower bound of the waiting competitors was not needed for witnesses and was not labelled, so "popped state provably not worse" could rarely be decided. The numbers are generated in `results/primary_tables.md`; the unresolved fraction is reported before any rate.

**5. What does WL do on the same states, and does that support an intervention?** On the F and R problems there is no certified DENSE witness to compare. On the exact-label controls the picture is the opposite of the hypothesis: WL has cross-group witnesses, DENSE has none, and DENSE's local rankings on provable pairs are correct on 244 of 246. That is evidence for DENSE's in-family strength (already known from the earlier cards), not for a cross-scope weakness, and it says nothing about the IPC failures. The two scorers' values at the same states were computed offline for the whole union of sampled states (3,207 DENSE and 3,207 WL values); the enqueue values used in the searches agree with the offline DENSE values to 2.4e-4 (GPU nondeterminism).

**6. Next step?** **Stop here; do not enter layer 2** (label is neither `CROSS_SCOPE_SIGNAL` nor `LOCAL_WITNESS...`). The inconclusive label is a statement about the measurement, not about the hypothesis: with a single reference plan from another producer and LM-cut as the only lower bound, the diagnostic has no power on the IPC problems where the question matters. The only alternative named by the plan (fixed-L2 WL exact-successor fit, CPU) is unstarted and needs a separate decision; any repeat of this diagnostic would need anchors from the scorer's own frontier or a tighter bound, i.e. a different card, and was not started.

## 4. Deviations, corrections and disclosures

- **Label correction after seeing results.** The registered configuration applied `min_bounded_events: 8` to events whose bounds *exist*; plan 14.1 says overlapping bounds are UNKNOWN. The first analysis pass therefore returned `NO_LOCALIZED_SIGNAL_IN_OBSERVED_PREFIX` although not a single event had a decided pair (first-pass files are kept in `results/first_pass_as_registered/`). I changed only the quantity the threshold applies to (events with at least one pair decided by non-overlapping bounds, `resolved_events`; threshold 8 unchanged) and skipped the trivial pair of a popped state with itself (the init state and states on the reference path are their own anchor). No trace, score or bound was changed; the correction moves the label toward the weaker claim, and neither label releases layer 2. Both labels are in `results/method_decision.json`.
- **Fixture searches: 24 booked against a cap of 8.** The fixture set (4 synthetic prefixes in `tests/test_scope_diag.py` plus WL and DENSE plain / observed on one control problem) ran three times: the first version used full solves of a small Train problem (> 16 expansions), the second failed on a test bug (competitor hash seeded with another case id), the third is the final one (all <= 16 expansions). All are tiny Train96 searches; the ledger `receipts/ledger.jsonl` lists every run.
- **GPU nondeterminism.** DENSE enqueue values and offline values differ by up to 2.4e-4; certificates and orders of the DENSE-driven prefixes use the enqueue values, the offline values only for cross-scorer columns. No trajectory-equivalence claim is made.
- **Machine load.** The shared machine had a load average of 11-16 on 112 cores; the DENSE prefixes of p12 and p17 hit the 60 s limit before 4,096 expansions (4,053 / 2,825) and have 29 / 28 of 30 planned snapshots.
- **Observer.** `gbfs_observed` is a copy of the engine with read-only bookkeeping; fixtures check identical pop sequence, plan and counters against `search_match/engine.gbfs` on synthetic and real runs (the DENSE check replays the recorded values of the plain search to separate GPU noise from an observer effect).
- **Code changed after the registration commit** (checked against `registration/source_identity.json`): `analysis.py` (the correction above and the generated table `primary_tables.md`), the report command of the driver script (decision file fields) and `tests/test_scope_diag.py` (new analysis tests). The observer, bound services, reference selection, trace / label / scoring stages, the engine and the configuration are byte-identical to their registered hashes.
