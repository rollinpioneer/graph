Rows 8 and 9 both apply, to different problems. The plan asks for one priority: the guidance-failure problems already ran 13k-29k expansions, so their failure is not a cost problem, and for p21 / p22 a 1.3-2x cost reduction cannot close a 14-95x gap. The single priority is therefore the topology-coverage training card; exact cost reduction is not chosen, independent confirmation is folded into the topology card (new independently generated problems).

One failure of one recipe (ALT) does not show that topology training will work, nor that the relational representation is at fault; the guidance gap on four IPC problems is the only evidence for the next step and its cause (topology, supervision, optimisation, scale) is a hypothesis.
