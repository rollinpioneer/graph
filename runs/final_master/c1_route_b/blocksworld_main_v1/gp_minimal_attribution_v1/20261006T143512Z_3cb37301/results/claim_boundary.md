# Claim boundary — C1-BW-GP-MINIMAL-ATTRIBUTION-V1

Permitted: under this fixed protocol (one training run per learned condition, single seed, fixed weights), adding the rank loss to the B2 path improved the no-destruction multi-tower layer (net +9 successes over M0) but not the must-destroy layer; restoring the encoder goal marks (M1-GOAL) left the multi-tower and must-destroy results of M1 unchanged and clearly reduced the old M1's RED-over-BLUE asymmetry on the colour-twin layer; the zero-training G1+C3 combination was stronger than every learned condition on this suite; G1 alone never solved a problem whose every optimal plan destroys a satisfied goal; the registered labels are scoped to ALL optimal plans (UNKNOWN never coerced).

Not permitted: that goal-free encoding, the rank loss, or any single component is "the" mechanism (two switches were tested separately, the rest of the bundle was not); that colour is, or is not, the cause of earlier regressions beyond the twin pairs observed (both-colour failures do not exclude colour acting with structure); that tower height is isolated (H4 differs from T6-2 in goal edges, layout and plan length as well); that the 5-/3-episode thresholds are significance tests, seed variability or equivalence bounds ("no clear difference" is not "equal"); that M1-GOAL or any learned model beats the simple rules; that G1+C3 is optimal or complete (it fails optimality on H4 and some other cells); that the learned models generalise beyond the frozen cells, to other seeds, or beyond Blocksworld; that this suite is blind for any design that follows from these results; any change to the earlier ID-gate failures or REP label; any statement about the aborted partial runs (not evaluated).
