# SAW-RD-01 conditional intervention decision

Status: NOT_EXECUTED. Frozen after discovery and before confirmation diagnosis outcomes on 2026-10-12 (Asia/Taipei).

Both seed-0 baselines completed 1M joint updates. Discovery matched-waypoint execution is 15/16 for both low_actor and actor in both datasets. Stitch's original-future vs registered-task-goal action matching means differ (.030809 vs 1.427157), and all 250 final initial state-goal pairs exceed the 99th-percentile distance of a finite 4096-training-probe reference. This identifies a goal-conditional coverage/generalization candidate, not a demonstrated causal failure of policy extraction.

Competing explanations: unreliable teachers for distant evaluation goals, saturated or poorly ranked weights, conflicting supported future actions, inadequate joint training support, and final-policy generalization. Candidate rho saturation is 51.56% in Stitch discovery. We have not demonstrated that a teacher action is both useful toward the distant evaluation goal and properly weighted with sufficient training support. Good execution toward a short waypoint is insufficient for that claim. The prerequisites for frozen-value/teacher actor-only training are not established. No extra training, oracle selection, extra seed or alternate algorithm will be launched.

The remaining ordinary S3 confirmation uses the already frozen sampler seeds 111001/111002 and official validation data, 32 state-goal groups per dataset and at most 16 same-waypoint pairs per dataset (two policies, at most 64 additional short segments). This is a diagnostic replication, not an S4 intervention. It uses the same 1M checkpoints, identical qpos/qvel resets per matched pair, original goal tolerance and recorded span capped at 25 steps. All samples, failures and groups without supported teacher targets are retained. Validation was used for training logs; it is not described as an untouched dataset. Confirmation outcomes will be reported even if they contradict discovery. No second intervention is permitted in this round.

Stopping decision: finish this confirmation, four-module matrix, evidence identities, resource accounting and branch archive; do not develop new modules. Local success gaps concern these fixed checkpoints and selected states only. Geometry and finite nearest-neighbor support proxies do not provide optimal-value truth or prove absence of full-data support.
