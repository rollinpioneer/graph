# Final Research Report

## 1. Research boundary

This M6 freeze is limited to the manual core reward representation. New training, data, main reward retuning, and test-driven revisions are forbidden by the final lock.

## 2. Core evidence

Independent checkpoint inference, raw-prediction reward recomputation, and content-group statistics support: path_consistency, failure_sign, recovery_sign, loop_safety, success_separation, remove_recovery, remove_debt_cap.

## 3. Qualified evidence

alternative_A_collapse, alternative_B_collapse, remove_phi

## 4. Negative and excluded results

stable policy improvement, coverage scaling generalization, unseen-order generalization, automatic graph discovery as main contribution, nonzero beta or eta as validated main components. Coverage and unseen-order extension claims remain removed; policy is secondary/mixed; automatic graph discovery is not the main method.

## 5. Reproducibility

The compact M6 index records all packaged evidence. The lightweight release provides checksum validation and external manifests for checkpoints, data, raw predictions, and distributions.

## 6. Recommended paper positioning

Position PathGraph-SARM as a bounded, manually specified graph-structured reward representation with explicit recovery semantics, not as a general policy-improvement, scaling, or automatic-graph result.
