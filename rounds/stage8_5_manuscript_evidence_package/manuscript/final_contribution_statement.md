# Final Contribution Statement

This work is limited to a locked manual, graph-structured reward representation. Its contribution is to encode multiple legal paths, explicit failure/recovery transitions, remaining-cost reduction plus within-node progress, and recovery-debt accounting. The final statistical evidence supports: path_consistency, failure_sign, recovery_sign, loop_safety, success_separation, remove_recovery, remove_debt_cap.

It does not claim stable policy improvement, coverage scaling, unseen-order generalization, or automatic graph discovery as a main contribution.
