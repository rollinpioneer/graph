# Reviewer Question Bank

## Why not use a linear stage chain?
The frozen manual graph represents two legal orders rather than collapsing them to one sequence; controlled evidence is qualified by its limited support.

## Why use a manual graph?
Manual graph structure remains the stated main method. Automatic graph exploration is appendix-only and is not claimed as a contribution.

## How does recovery avoid reward hacking?
Recovery credit is bounded by accumulated failure debt; the locked configuration has eta=0 and beta=0, so nonzero loop and uncertainty terms are not claimed.

## Why is policy not a main conclusion?
The frozen policy evidence is secondary/mixed, not stable across seeds.

## Why not claim unseen-order generalization?
The real order-holdout rerun did not meet the predeclared extension support condition.

## What are the data limitations?
The task/data coverage is finite; controlled symbolic stress is not real-robot generalization.

## How does this differ from planners or stage-transition rewards?
The scope is a graph-structured reward representation evaluated on the locked benchmark, not a claim about a general planner or a universally superior transition reward.
